# Changelog

## 1.28
- Choose a style for the sensors over your image or GIF on the Kraken LCD: Bar, Gauge, Dual or Minimal.
- GPU temperature and load are shown on the Kraken LCD when available (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel).

## 1.27
- LEDs always turn off when Windows shuts down. The setting now only applies to sleep.

## 1.26
- New "What's new" button in Settings shows a summary of every update since your version.
- The update message no longer shows a raw GitHub link.

## 1.25
- Motherboard RGB (Gigabyte and MSI) now also turns off its stored effect on shutdown and sleep, so fans on motherboard headers stay dark while the PC is off.

## 1.24
- More reliable LED blackout when Windows shuts down: LiteRGB is notified earlier and listens to more shutdown signals.
- The log now shows when Windows shuts down and when the LEDs were turned off.

## 1.23
- LiteRGB now shows up in Task Manager under Startup apps, also when it starts as administrator.
- Disabling it in Task Manager is respected.
- The exe now has a name and publisher in Windows.

## 1.22
- Autostart is more reliable and repairs itself when the exe is moved.
- New option to start as administrator without a UAC prompt.

## 1.21
- Unchanged NZXT colors are resent once a minute instead of every 2 seconds.

## 1.20
- GIFs in the search window are animated.
- Search results load faster.

## 1.19
- GIF search works without your own API key.

## 1.18
- Search GIFs (GIPHY) for the Kraken LCD and the Ajazz screen.
- Animated preview of the image on the Ajazz screen tab.
